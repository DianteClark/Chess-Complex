Privacy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version

Chess Complex has no accounts and does not collect personal information. Your progress lives on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your player name, loadout, unlocked cosmetics, Marks, XP, quests, achievements and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data erases them. You can back them up from Settings using a backup code.

Online play

Friend-code matches connect players directly to each other (peer to peer, using WebRTC). To find each other, browsers briefly contact a public matchmaking (signalling) server operated by PeerJS, and public STUN servers, which can see your IP address as part of making the connection. During a match, the players in your room receive your chosen name, cosmetics and game moves. Nothing is stored after the match.

Third-party resources

The game loads fonts from Google Fonts and the PeerJS library from unpkg.com. Those services may log standard request information such as your IP address under their own policies.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
