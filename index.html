      How to Play

      Last piece standing

      Everyone starts as a Pawn on a 16 by 16 board, up to 8 players. Eliminate everyone else. Before round 6, a piece at full HP survives any single hit (Steadfast), so nobody is knocked out in the opening.

      Turns run on speed

      Every piece has a speed meter. Higher SPD means you act more often. The strip at the top shows who moves next. On your turn you get one move and one action, in either order. Vault and Sprint are free actions.

      Evolve

      Collect Promotion Shards from the floor, crates, Hollows and fallen players, then tap Promote. The ladder is Pawn, then Knight or Bishop (2 shards), then Rook (4 more), then Queen (7 more), and promoting never uses your action. Or Hone your current piece instead: +15% stats and a stronger Signature per level, up to 3, so you can stay your favourite piece all game.

      Attunement

      Every shard you pick up also attunes you for the rest of the match: +1% HP, +0.8% ATK and +0.5% DEF per shard, plus +1 SPD every 6 shards, up to 30. Spending shards never removes attunement, so keep collecting even after you max out your piece. Every 5 attunement adds an orbiting crystal so everyone can see how strong you are.

      Ultimates

      Dealing and taking damage charges your ultimate. When the gold button is full, unleash your piece's ultimate: En Passant, Fork, Excommunicate, Castling, Checkmate Nova, or the King's Divine Right.

      World events

      Every third round something happens: Shard Rain, a Supply Drop cache worth breaking open, an Earthquake that cracks walls, a Mana Surge that charges every ultimate, or War Drums that speed everyone up. From round 6 the dangerous ones join in: a Meteor Storm (get off the marked tiles), a Blood Moon that boosts all damage, an Eclipse that hides distant pieces, a Royal Bounty on the leader, and the Restless Dead.

      Hazards

      Most boards roll a hazard theme: lava, ice, portals, geyser vents that erupt every third round, conveyor tracks that carry pieces, cracked floor that collapses on the second step, or a rift storm that swaps two players. Chaos stacks two at once.

      Ten ways to play

      Battle Royale: the full game. Blitz: 15-second turns and a faster storm. Duel: one on one in a 10 by 10 arena. Squads: teams of two who can revive each other. Siege: co-op against eight waves of Hollows. Daily Challenge: one map and build for everyone, new each day. King of the Hill: start your turn on the golden Throne to score, first to 10. Chaos: two hazards and a world event every round. Gauntlet: a co-op boss rush that ends with the Regent Ascendant and a Colossus together. Mirror Match: everyone gets the same random build.

      The board

      Walls block movement and shots. Rubble and crates break when hit. Brambles stop slides and cost initiative. Shrines heal 40% once, then recharge. Hollows guard shard caches.

      The storm

      From round 5 the outer ring cracks, then collapses a round later, and the storm closes in again every 3 rounds. The void burns 20% of your max HP at the start of each of your turns there. You can still move and act, so get out fast. Once the board is fully closed, everyone burns 6% a turn.

      The Crown

      On round 7 the Crown appears on a random safe tile. Whoever takes it becomes the King: more HP and healing every turn, but slow single steps, and everyone will hunt you. Kill the King and the Crown shatters into 4 shards.

      Controls

      Tap a highlighted tile to move. Pick an ability, tap a target to preview the damage, tap again to confirm. Hold an action button to see what it does. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. On a keyboard: 1-4 for actions, P to promote, Space or E to end your turn, Esc to cancel.
