      How to Play

      Last piece standing

      Everyone starts as a Pawn on a 16 by 16 board. Up to 8 players. Eliminate everyone else.

      Turns run on speed

      Every piece has a speed meter. Higher SPD means you act more often. The strip at the top shows who moves next. On your turn you get one move and one action, in either order. Vault and Sprint are free actions.

      Evolve

      Collect Promotion Shards from the floor, crates, Hollows and fallen players. Spend them to evolve: Pawn, then Knight or Bishop, then Rook, then Queen. Or Hone your current piece for +15% stats and a stronger signature, so you can stay your favourite piece all game.

      Attunement

      Every shard you pick up also attunes you, permanently: +1% HP, +0.8% ATK and +0.5% DEF per shard, plus +1 SPD every 6 shards, up to 30. Spending shards never removes attunement, so keep collecting even after you max out your piece. Every 5 attunement adds an orbiting crystal so everyone can see how strong you are.

      Ultimates

      Dealing and taking damage charges your Ultimate. When the gold button is full, unleash your piece's ultimate: En Passant, Fork, Excommunicate, Castling, Checkmate Nova, or the King's Divine Right.

      World events

      Every third round something happens: Shard Rain, a Meteor Storm (get off the marked tiles), a Blood Moon that boosts all damage, or a Supply Drop cache worth breaking open.

      The board

      Walls block movement and shots. Rubble and crates break. Brambles stop slides and slow you down. Shrines heal once, then recharge. Hollows guard shard caches.

      The storm

      From round 5 the outer ring cracks, then collapses a round later. Standing in the void burns 20% of your HP every turn. Once the board is fully closed, everyone burns.

      The Crown

      On round 7 the Crown appears at the centre. Whoever takes it becomes the King: more HP, steady healing, but slow single steps. Kill the King and the Crown drops.

      Controls

      Tap a highlighted tile to move. Pick an ability, tap a target to preview the damage, tap again to confirm. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. On a keyboard: 1-4 for actions, P to promote, Space to end your turn.
